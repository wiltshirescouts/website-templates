Jamboree Alternative 2027

A two-week international adventure for Scouts, Explorers and volunteers from across Wiltshire. Read how it works, then sign up in about five minutes.

Adult sign-up Young person sign-up

Who can take part

The trip is open to Scouts and Explorers aged 14 to 17 on 1 August 2027, and to adult volunteers who are at least 18. You do not need to have been abroad with Scouts before.

  • Young people need a parent or carer’s consent.
  • Adults must hold a current Disclosure and Barring Service check through Scouts.
  • Everyone completes the health and dietary form before the deposit is due.

Deposit

A £150 deposit secures a place. The trip team send payment details separately. No payment is taken through this website.

How places are decided

The trip team review every sign-up and build a balanced group. You will hear the outcome by email within two weeks of the closing date. Your place is provisional until the trip team confirm it.
