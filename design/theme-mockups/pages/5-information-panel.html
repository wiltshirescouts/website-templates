Jamboree Alternative

JAWS (Jamboree Alternative Wiltshire Scouts) is an international adventure for young people across Wiltshire, running alongside the 2027 World Scout Jamboree.

You’ll spend a week at an international Scout centre in the mountains, taking on new challenges, building independence and making friends with Scouts from around the world.

The programme is still being planned. Watch the video below for a taste of what’s on offer, and check whether you’re eligible on the sign-up form.
